## Graduate Research Assistant

**University of Virginia, Link Lab** | Charlottesville, VA | Aug 2022-present

* Built an open-source, synchronized multimodal data collection system for human-subject studies, combining egocentric video and audio, smartwatch motion data, custom time-of-flight sensing, and medical-device ground truth.
* Led the collection and release of [EgoEMS](https://uva-dsa.github.io/EgoEMS/): over 20 hours of data across 233 simulated emergency scenarios with 62 participants, including 46 EMS professionals.
* Co-developed [MiDAS](https://doi.org/10.1002/rcs.70220), a platform-agnostic multimodal acquisition system for robot-assisted surgery, validated on Raven-II and da Vinci Xi.
* Developed multimodal activity recognition methods for EMS cognitive assistance and real-time surgical activity prediction and safety monitoring.

**Tools:** Python, PyTorch, C++, CUDA, Azure Kinect SDK, Android, Java, Vuzix AR glasses.
